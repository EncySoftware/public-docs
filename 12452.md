**Hot keys:**

***Space:*** Hit Space to switch between drawing lines and arcs.

***Shift***: Hold Shift and click on an arc or a circle to make the line tangent to it.

***Trim a contour*** Hold and drag the left mouse button to trim out a piece of a contour.

***RMB***: Exit.

**Arc by two points.** Draw an arc connecting two points.

1. Click the first point of the arc.

2. Click the second point of the arc.

3. Either click the third point on the arc or enter the radius of the arc in the Radius field.

![images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-20_8-52-8.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-20_8-52-8.png)

**Radius.** Predict the radius between two points.

**Start point.** Allows you to specify point by coordinates.

**End point.** Allows you to specify point by coordinates.

**Hot keys:**

**Shift**: Hold Shift and click on an arc or a line to make the arc tangent to it.

**RMB**: Exit.

**Arc from center.** Draw an arc starting with the center point

1. Click the center point of the arc. 2. Click the first point of the arc. 3. Click the second point of the arc.

![images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-20_8-52-29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-20_8-52-29.png)

**Hot keys:**

**Hold Shift and click** on a segment to make the circle tangent to the segment.

**Hold Ctrl** to create multiple circles with the same diameter.

Draw a circle tangent to another geometry

**Hold Shift and click** on lines, arcs, circles or points to draw a circle tangent to those entities.

Enter the size of the circle in the field above when needed.

**RMB**: Exit.

1. Click the first point of the slot.

2. Click the second point of the slot.

3. Click the periferal point of the slot to define the slot width.

### Features of working with drawings.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**When working in the drawing mode and selecting curves (Left or right mouse button), various settings are possible (changing the line type, restrictions, etc.)**

In drawing mode, you can select 3 main lines:

- **Main** - Used for drawing a part and its visible sides.

![images/download/thumbnails/157860785/image2025-5-21_14-11-12.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157860785/image2025-5-21_14-11-12.png)

- **Construction** - Image of invisible outlines of an object

![images/download/thumbnails/157860785/image2025-5-21_14-11-39.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157860785/image2025-5-21_14-11-39.png)

- **Center line** - Designation of axial lines and center lines of circles

![images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-21_14-12-3.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-21_14-12-3.png)

**Lock position.** Allows you to enter restrictions (angle, size, etc.) The object does not move in space

**Lock agle**. Allows you to limit the angle of an element

**Make horizontal.** Allows you to make the curve horizontal. The curve can be part of the contour

**Center lines.** Allows you to set the centerlines of a circle/radius

**Dimension.** Allows you to set the dimensions. As a rule, the size is set from the 0 point of the drawing to the element.

**Defeature.** Allows you to exclude an element (for compound figures, contours, etc.)

**When selecting multiple elements (for example 2 segments), the following restrictions are available:**

**The restriction can be removed by selecting the restriction sign and pressing the DEL key.**

**Equal.** Introduces a length limit (two segments become equal). They are denoted by the **equal sign**. T

![images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-27-55.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-27-55.png)

**Parallel.** Makes two elements parallel to each other. The limit sign is visible when one of the elements is selected.

![images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-32-55.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-32-55.png)

**Perpendicular.** Makes two elements perpendicular to each other.

![images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-37-12.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-37-12.png)

**Colinear.** Allows you to set 2 curves on 1 line.

![images/download/thumbnails/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-42-22.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-42-22.png) ![images/download/thumbnails/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-42-32.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157860785/image2025-5-21_12-42-32.png)
