# Block parameterization

Before combining the elements into a block, enter the values you need in the <strong>[Title]</strong> fields of the sizes.

![images/download/attachments/157860468/image2024-8-9_17-52-34.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860468/image2024-8-9_17-52-34.png)

After merging the elements, these parameters will appear in the inspector and will be available for editing.

![images/download/attachments/157860468/image2024-8-9_17-53-26.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860468/image2024-8-9_17-53-26.png)
