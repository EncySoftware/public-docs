# Arc with center, first point and angle

![images/download/attachments/157860187/image2024-8-7_17-13-59.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860187/image2024-8-7_17-13-59.png)

To construct an arc, you must consistently specify the center, first and second points of the arc.

**Center.** X | Y coordinates.

**P1.** The first arc point.

**Radius**, **Diameter**. Arc size.

**Angle.** Entered instead of P2.

Guides are offered from the center (after specifying it), and then additionally from the first point of the arc.
