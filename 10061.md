## Application area:

The Extract Isolines tool extracts isolines from a surface. It activates after selecting a face on the <strong>Model tab</strong>. The editing panel lets you create a spline, change the isoline direction, or split the face. Use the <strong>Measure tool</strong> for precise spline positioning.

- ![images/download/thumbnails/157860026/Add_spline_24.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157860026/Add_spline_24.png) to form a spline from the isoline.

![images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-10-29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-10-29.png) ![images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-10-11.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-10-11.png)

- ![images/download/thumbnails/157860026/Change_direction_24.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157860026/Change_direction_24.png) to change a isoline direction.

![images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-12-5.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-12-5.png) ![images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-12-35.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-12-35.png)

- ![images/download/thumbnails/157860026/Split_faces_24.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157860026/Split_faces_24.png) split one face into two, with or without preserving the common (not split) face

![images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-14-9.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-14-9.png) ![images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-13-24.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860026/image2026-4-14_9-13-24.png)

- Use the <strong>Measure tool</strong> to accurately define a spline. You can move the point to any location on the part to bind it to a specific position. Once the endpoint is anchored, you can modify the spline's position.
