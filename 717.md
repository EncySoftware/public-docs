# Geometrical model preparation

The geometrical model preparation mode becomes activated by the mouse click onto the [Model](86.md) tab in the system's main window.

In the **Model** mode one can:

- to [import](109.md) geometrical data (CAD) files;
- to [correct the structure](47.md) of the geometrical model;
- to perform [spatial transformations](116.md) of the geometrical objects;
- to generate new elements from existing ones;
- to [alter the object's visual properties](32.md);

Access to elements of the model is performed both from the [model tree window](718.md) and interactively on the screen. Different functions can be launched via the pop up menu in the [graphical window](169.md) and the model tree window. Buttons for frequently used functions are put on the toolbar of the **Model** tab.
