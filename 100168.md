# Holes

## Application area:

The <strong>Holes</strong> group provides operations for machining holes and generating machining processes from recognized manufacturing features.

### Operations:

- <strong>Hole machining.</strong> Creates hole machining toolpaths, including drilling, boring, and threading cycles. [Details](10100.md)

- <strong>FBM Mill.</strong> Generates operations for recognized manufacturing features using machining procedures. [Details](10089.md)
