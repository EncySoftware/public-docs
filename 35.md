# Process indicator

The **Process indicator** runs when the system performs lengthy operations, such as geometrical model import, toolpath calculation, machining simulation, etc. Left-clicking on the indicator cancels these operations, but only after a confirmation prompt.

The **Process indicator** is located at the bottom of the main window.
