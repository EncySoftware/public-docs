# Legacy operations

## Application area:

This section provides reference information for legacy machining operations, including 2.5D chamfering, cover machining, pocketing, wall machining, finishing plane, optimized plane, complex machining, combined waterline-drive machining, drive finishing and roughing, and sawing.

Use these topics when reviewing or modifying a project that contains one of these operations. Each topic describes the operation's purpose, geometry requirements, and machining parameters, helping to explain how its toolpath is generated.

The following operations are also documented in this group:

- <strong>Finishing plane.</strong> Generates surface finishing passes in parallel planes. [Details](713.md)
- <strong>Optimized plane.</strong> Combines two perpendicular plane strategies, with each strategy machining areas selected by their surface slope. [Details](714.md)
- <strong>Complex.</strong> Combines plane and waterline finishing for flat and steep areas. [Details](839.md)
