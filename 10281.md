# Disc roughing

![Roughing stone with a saw blade](assets/images/editor/10281/e724428763324b3e98f4607c74619e88.png)

## Application area:

The operation prepares stone for finishing by making cuts with a saw blade. The thin webs of material between the cuts are then removed manually by spalling, followed by finishing with an appropriate tool.

The primary tool is a **saw blade**. The operation also supports end mills and undercut tools. The descriptions and illustrations below refer to a saw blade.

### Setup:

The **Setup** tab defines the part position on the machine, the coordinate system, and the main project parameters. [Details](1022.md)

### Job assignment:

The model to be machined is defined in the operation’s **Part**. The job assignment defines machining levels and restrictions.

**Top level.** Sets the upper machining limit using a selected geometric element. [Details](10153.md)

### Strategy:

#### Toolpath:

Defines the arrangement of the cuts.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**Rotary.** Cuts are generated around the rotary axis and distributed along it.

![Rotary](assets/images/editor/10281/c1b34b9fedde46ebbfdf2593348fbc6a.png)

**3d.** Parallel cuts are generated in a plane.

![3d](assets/images/editor/10281/5317daf14ddc443b960834a15a21f9f6.png)

**Step.** The distance between adjacent cuts (strings). Enter a distance or a percentage of the disc diameter; the default is 20%. If the axial zone is not an exact multiple of the step, the step is reduced to distribute the cuts evenly.

![Step](assets/images/editor/10281/64dae4804b454f4b9e42bd3c7ef6e13f.png)

**Angle.** Sets the cut direction in the plane, measured from the X axis. Available only for **3d**.

![Angle](assets/images/editor/10281/7fa8d953061645b0b52735d5eacda028.png)

**Change side.** Reverses the tool axis and shifts the toolpath point by the disc thickness while preserving the cut position.

**ON.** The tool is positioned on the opposite side of the cut.

#### Roughing:

Defines how material is removed within each cut.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**By layers.** Machining uses separate layers at the specified depth of cut. The shape of the turns relative to the part and workpiece depends on **Workpiece**.

![By layers](assets/images/disc-roughing/roughing-by-layers.png)

**Adaptive.** Material is removed along a spiral while limiting the width of cut. Rounding radii control the smoothness of roughing passes, links, and toolpath corners.

![Adaptive](assets/images/disc-roughing/roughing-adaptive.png)

**Depth of cut.** The distance between adjacent layers within a cut. Enter a distance or a percentage of the disc diameter; the default is 100%.

![Depth of cut](assets/images/disc-roughing/depth-of-cut.png)

**Workpiece.** A parameter within **Roughing** on the **Strategy** tab. It is available only when **Roughing** is set to **By layers** and determines how the turns of each layer are generated relative to the workpiece. With **Adaptive**, the parameter is hidden and the workpiece is always taken into account.

<details open="">
<summary>Click here to expand...</summary>

- **Use workpiece.** The default mode. A turn is generated only where workpiece material is present. Where the part rises above the layer, the turn is interrupted. The resulting segments are connected by links with approaches and retracts.

  ![Layer turn interrupted where the part rises above the layer](assets/images/disc-roughing/use-workpiece.png)

- **Ignore workpiece.** The workpiece and the tool cover are not taken into account when generating turns. Each turn is a continuous pass along its entire length: it follows the layer where the part is lower and the part surface where the part is higher. There are no breaks or retracts within a turn. The layers are limited only by the top machining level, rather than the top of the workpiece, so some turns may pass through empty space.

  ![Continuous layer turn following the part surface](assets/images/disc-roughing/ignore-workpiece.png)

- **Link feed outside.** The turns are continuous, as in **Ignore workpiece** mode. Where no workpiece material is present, the tool moves at the short link feed; within the material, it moves at the working feed. Only the feed changes at the material boundary, without a retract. In the illustration, blue lines indicate the working feed and the orange dashed line indicates the short link feed.

  ![Continuous turn with the short link feed outside the workpiece](assets/images/disc-roughing/link-feed-outside.png)

**Minimum cut depth.** A parameter within **Roughing**, available only when **Workpiece** is set to **Ignore workpiece** or **Link feed outside**. The default value is 0.1 mm. The cut depth of a turn is the distance from the top of its layer to the lowest point of the part along that turn. Turns with a cut depth below the specified value are not generated. This removes lower turns that follow the part surface almost entirely and remove very little material. A value of 0 allows all turns to be generated.

![Minimum cut depth excluding the bottom layer turn](assets/images/disc-roughing/minimum-cut-depth.png)

</details>

The following parameters are available only with **Adaptive** roughing.

**Corners smoothing.** The radius used to smooth toolpath corners near the part surface.

![Corners smoothing](assets/images/disc-roughing/corners-smoothing.png)

**Rough rounding radius.** The rounding radius for roughing passes.

![Rough rounding radius](assets/images/disc-roughing/rough-rounding-radius.png)

**Linking radius.** The radius of arcs connecting the passes. It must be at least the safe distance; the inspector marks an invalid value as an error.

![Linking radius](assets/images/disc-roughing/linking-radius.png)

</details>

#### Job zone:

Limits machining along the rotary axis and within an angular range. Available only for **Rotary** machining.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**Rotary axis.** Set the axis in the inspector or with the mouse. **Orientation** follows X, Y, or Z of the base coordinate system, or a custom direction; **Origin** defines the axis position. **Base CS** is **Global CS** or **WCS**.

![Rotary axis](assets/images/download/attachments/157864075/Rotary_axis.png)

**Min. axial position.** The lower axial limit: **Auto** or a value measured from the rotary axis origin.

![Min. axial position](assets/images/download/attachments/157864100/Min._axial_position.png)

**Max. axial position.** The upper axial limit: **Auto** or a value measured from the rotary axis origin.

![Max. axial position](assets/images/download/attachments/157864100/Min._axial_position.png)

**Angular domain.** Selects a full revolution or a sector between the start and finish angles. Adjust the limits by dragging or entering a value beside the dimension line.

**Full circle.** Turns extend around the full circle.

![Full circle](assets/images/disc-roughing/angular-domain-full-circle.png)

**Sector.** Turns are limited to the specified angular range.

![Sector](assets/images/disc-roughing/angular-domain-sector.png)

**Start angle.** The start of the sector, measured from the zero-angle direction.

![Start angle](assets/images/disc-roughing/start-angle.png)

**Finish angle.** The end of the sector, measured from the zero-angle direction.

![Finish angle](assets/images/disc-roughing/finish-angle.png)

</details>

#### Sorting:

**Toolpath uniting mode** defines the order in which strings and layers are machined.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**By layers.** Each cut is machined to its full depth before the next cut is started.

![By layers](assets/images/download/attachments/157864075/image2024-4-17_12-10-18.png)

**By strings.** One layer is machined across all cuts before the next layer is started. Within a layer, machining proceeds region by region, completing one region before moving to the next.

![By strings](assets/images/download/attachments/157864075/image2024-4-17_12-10-36.png)

With **Link feed outside**, the layer is not split into regions: the turn remains continuous.

**Reverse odd strings.** Reverses the machining direction of every second cut: the 2nd, 4th, 6th, and so on.

**OFF.** Adjacent strings follow the same direction.

![OFF](assets/images/editor/10281/384eccda9d1f4d4995252d98f0a83f1a.png)

**ON.** Adjacent strings alternate direction.

![ON](assets/images/editor/10281/f69a2cfdeda149b7bddf3a751f8c00ee.png)

</details>

#### Machining levels:

For **Rotary** machining, levels are distances from the rotary axis, not Z heights.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**Top level.** The upper limit of the machining layers. Available only with **By layers** roughing.

![Top level](assets/images/download/attachments/157864075/Top_radius.png)

**Bottom level.** The lower machining limit; no turns are generated below it.

![Bottom level](assets/images/download/attachments/157864075/Bottom_radius.png)

</details>
