<p>Next, you need to add a Turret head node.</p>
<p><a class="doc-image-link" href="images/download/attachments/131898663/image2024-5-2_10-29-5.png" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="Open full-size image"><img alt="images/download/attachments/131898663/image2024-5-2_10-29-5.png" class="confluence-embedded-image confluence-content-image-border doc-image" src="images/download/attachments/131898663/image2024-5-2_10-29-5.png" style="--doc-image-width:768px"/></a>
</p>
<p>It is important to position the Base CS on the back of the mechanism.</p>
<p><a class="doc-image-link" href="images/download/attachments/131898663/CSTURRET.gif" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="Open full-size image"><img alt="images/download/attachments/131898663/CSTURRET.gif" class="confluence-embedded-image doc-image" src="images/download/attachments/131898663/CSTURRET.gif" style="--doc-image-width:800px"/></a>
</p>
<p>Next, you need to position the connection points of the blocks. To do this, you can use Interactive mode, which helps you to perform this task quickly. By dragging 1 point it automatically sets other points as well.</p>
<p><a class="doc-image-link" href="images/download/attachments/131898663/interturret.gif" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="Open full-size image"><img alt="images/download/attachments/131898663/interturret.gif" class="confluence-embedded-image doc-image" src="images/download/attachments/131898663/interturret.gif" style="--doc-image-width:800px"/></a>
</p>
<p>The next step is to place the tool blocks on the connecting points. You can add tools in 2 ways:<br/>1) Use the right mouse button;<br/>2) Select the desired tool from the drop-down menu.</p>
<p><a class="doc-image-link" href="images/download/attachments/131898663/nakonectoturretheadkrug.gif" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="Open full-size image"><img alt="images/download/attachments/131898663/nakonectoturretheadkrug.gif" class="confluence-embedded-image doc-image" height="553" src="images/download/attachments/131898663/nakonectoturretheadkrug.gif" style="--doc-image-width:800px" width="800"/></a>
</p>
<p>On the Simulation tab make sure that the Turret head moves correctly and click apply.</p>
<p><a class="doc-image-link" href="images/download/attachments/131898663/applyturrethead.gif" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="Open full-size image"><img alt="images/download/attachments/131898663/applyturrethead.gif" class="confluence-embedded-image doc-image" src="images/download/attachments/131898663/applyturrethead.gif" style="--doc-image-width:800px"/></a>
</p>
<p>At this point, the build of the lathe milling machine is complete.</p>
