<h1 class="heading"><span>Supported mechanism types</span></h1>
<ul class=""><li class=""><p><strong class="">Turret Head Node</strong> - a component enabling tool rotation and positioning during operation, often facilitating tool fixation, tool change, and positional data processing.</p>
</li><li class=""><p><strong class="">Chuck Node</strong> - a component for securing workpieces or tools during machining, typically incorporating clamping mechanisms such as collet chucks or hydraulic clamps, with control and adjustment features.</p>
</li><li class=""><p><strong class="">Tool Block</strong> - a component for mounting and supporting cutting tools during material processing, designed for various tool types like drills, mills, and inserts, usually equipped with quick-change and adjustment mechanisms.</p>
</li><li class=""><p><strong class="">Movable Tool Block</strong> - a device capable of spatial movement to precisely position tools or workpieces during machining operations, often movable along different axes and controlled by mechanical, hydraulic, or electrical systems.</p>
</li></ul><p></p>
