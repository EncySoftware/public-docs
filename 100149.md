# Entradas e saídas para operações de revestimento

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads.png)

<strong>Sl</strong> - Safe level (nível seguro)

<strong>LD</strong> - Leads (entradas e saídas)

## Área de aplicação:

Permite adicionar entradas e saídas adicionais aos movimentos de trabalho, executadas com o avanço desses movimentos.

<strong>Engage.</strong>

<details>
<summary>Clique aqui para expandir..</summary>

<strong>Off.</strong> As partes de entrada e saída do percurso da ferramenta não são geradas.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_%28Off%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_(Off).png)

<strong>By Line.</strong> A entrada é executada com um ângulo em relação à vertical até o primeiro ponto de usinagem.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_%28Angle_to_Z_Axis%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_(Angle_to_Z_Axis).png)

<strong>By Arc.</strong> O sistema adiciona um arco ao primeiro ponto da curva usando o raio definido. O ângulo do arco é construído a partir da tangente da passada de trabalho.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_%28By_Arc_%28Angle%29%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_(By_Arc_(Angle)).png)

<strong>Length (Radius).</strong> Altera o comprimento.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_%28Length%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_(Length).png) ![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_%28Radius%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_(Radius).png)

<strong>Angle.</strong> Altera o ângulo em relação à vertical.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_%28Angle%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Engage_(Angle).png)

</details>

<strong>Retract.</strong>

<details>
<summary>Clique aqui para expandir..</summary>

<strong>Off.</strong> As partes de entrada e saída do percurso da ferramenta não são geradas.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_%28Off%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_(Off).png)

<strong>By Line.</strong> A saída é executada com um ângulo em relação à vertical a partir do último ponto de usinagem.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_%28Angle_to_Z_Axis%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_(Angle_to_Z_Axis).png)

<strong>By Arc.</strong> O sistema adiciona um arco ao último ponto da curva usando o raio definido. O ângulo do arco é construído a partir da tangente da passada de trabalho.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_%28By_Arc_%28Angle%29%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_(By_Arc_(Angle)).png)

<strong>Length (Radius).</strong> Altera o comprimento.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_%28Length%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_(Length).png) ![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_%28Radius%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_(Radius).png)

<strong>Angle.</strong> Altera o ângulo em relação à vertical.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_%28Angle%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Leads_Retrack_(Angle).png)

</details>

<strong>Use leads for.</strong>

<details>
<summary>Clique aqui para expandir..</summary>

<strong>Long links.</strong> As entradas e saídas são usadas apenas nas ligações longas.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Use_leads_for_Long_links.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Use_leads_for_Long_links.png)

<strong>Long and Short links.</strong> As entradas e saídas são usadas tanto nas ligações longas quanto nas curtas.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Use_leads_for__Long_and_Short_links.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Use_leads_for__Long_and_Short_links.png)

</details>

<strong>Overlaps.</strong>

<details>
<summary>Clique aqui para expandir..</summary>

<strong>Entry overlap.</strong> Permite definir uma sobreposição no início.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Curve_cladding_Entry_overlap.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Curve_cladding_Entry_overlap.png)

<strong>Exit overlap.</strong> Permite definir uma sobreposição no final.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Curve_cladding_Exit_overlap.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Curve_cladding_Exit_overlap.png)

</details>

<strong>Use leads for short links. Enable:</strong> Quando ativado, as entradas e saídas são adicionadas a todas as ligações, inclusive às curtas.

<strong>Disable:</strong> Quando desativado, as entradas e saídas são adicionadas apenas às ligações longas e às ligações de entrada/saída.

![images/download/attachments/162956963/Use_leads_for_short_links_%28off%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Use_leads_for_short_links_(off).png) ![images/download/attachments/162956963/Use_leads_for_short_links_%28on%29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/162956963/Use_leads_for_short_links_(on).png)
