# 2D Geometry sketcher

The **2D Geometry sketcher** provides tools for creating and editing planar geometry in the CAM system. It supports lines, arcs, circles, rectangles, and contours, together with tools for adding chamfers, rounding corners, offsetting, trimming, and splitting elements.

This section explains the common building and editing workflow and the settings of individual sketching tools. [Details](20000.md) It also covers dimensions, geometric constraints, transformations, and reusable blocks. Use these topics to construct and modify drawings and contours, either interactively or by entering coordinates and parameter values.
