# Zoom extents

For quick scaling of the view to display all visible objects, use the ![images/download/thumbnails/157858327/Zoom_extence_25.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157858327/Zoom_extence_25.png) button or the <strong>Fit Window</strong> item from the pop-up menu of the graphic screen.

![images/download/attachments/157858327/image2024-6-27_16-23-53.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157858327/image2024-6-27_16-23-53.png)

This function can also be activated by double-clicking the <strong>[Middle Mouse Button]</strong>, but it will also activate the closest [standard view](728.md).
