# Interactive rotate

Press and hold the [**Right Mouse Button**], then drag on the main visualization screen to rotate. Alternatively, use the **Rotate View** item from the pop-up menu of the graphic window to activate the rotate view mode.

![images/download/attachments/157858311/image2024-6-27_16-19-40.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157858311/image2024-6-27_16-19-40.png)

Drag up and down to rotate around the horizontal axis; drag left and right to rotate around the vertical axis.

Press and hold the **[X], [Y], or [Z]** key, and drag the mouse to rotate around the X, Y, or Z axis of the active coordinate system.

Hold **[Space]** while rotating to loop the rotation and animate it continuously. Press the **[Esc]** key to exit the continuous rotation mode.

Changing the view vector is also possible via the [Standard views](728.md) button.

Clicking the [**Middle Mouse Button**] (Mouse Wheel) in the graphic window performs a complete rotation of the current view vector to the nearest standard view.

The software also supports various 3D mouse devices, such as the SpaceNavigator.
