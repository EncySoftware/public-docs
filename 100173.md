# Turbomachinery Milling

## Application area:

The **Turbomachinery Milling** group contains specialized operations for machining impellers and individual blades. The operation topics describe the required blade and hub geometry, machining strategies, and toolpath settings.

### Operations:

- **Impeller Machining.** Provides roughing and finishing strategies for impeller blades, hubs, and fillets. [Details](1041.md)

- **Blade Machining.** Finishes blade airfoils, root zones, hubs, and edges. [Details](100127.md)
