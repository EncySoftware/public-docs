# Arc with three tangents

![images/download/attachments/157860194/image2024-8-7_17-15-44.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860194/image2024-8-7_17-15-44.png)

To construct an arc, you must sequentially specify three objects to which the arc should be tangent, with the **[Ctrl]** key pressed

![images/download/attachments/157860194/image2024-8-7_17-16-30.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860194/image2024-8-7_17-16-30.png)

Or specify two objects and enter the radius of the arc:

![images/download/attachments/157860194/image2024-8-7_17-17-55.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860194/image2024-8-7_17-17-55.png)

Points, lines, arcs and circles can be combined in any order. Clicking without **[Ctrl]** specifies a point, allowing construction through three points. A radius can replace the third element.
