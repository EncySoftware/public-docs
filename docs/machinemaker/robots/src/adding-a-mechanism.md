<div class="section section-1" id="src-129930542_id-.Addingamechanismv18-Supportedmechanismtypes">
<h1 class="heading"><span>Supported mechanism types</span></h1>
<ul class=""><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Robot </strong>- common industrial 6-axes robotic arm.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Heavy-Duty Robot </strong>- high payload robot with additional joints.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Cobot </strong>- collaborative 6-axes robotic arm.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">End effector</strong> -     <span style="color: #000000;">
device at the extremity of the robotic arm, designed to interact with the environment. Spindles, Laser Heads, Wire Cutters and etc. Robot can contain any number of end effectors. End effector can be added only after the robot is selected.    </span>
</span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p><strong class=""> <span style="color: #000000;">
Composite End effector     </span>
</strong> <span style="color: #000000;">
-    </span>
<span style="color: #000000;">
 complex devices, contain movable parts. For example gripper with fingers.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Fixed object</strong> - any fixed object (controller, enclosure, etc).    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Table (fixed)</strong> - static table    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Table (1 axis)</strong> - rotary table (positioner) with     <span style="color: #000000;">
1     </span>
axis    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class=""><strong class="">Table (</strong>2 axes) </strong>- rotary table (positioner) with     <span style="color: #000000;">
2     </span>
axes    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Rail</strong> - rail or any another linear axis. Usually allows you to move the robot along the X axis.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Portal (2 axes)</strong> - mechanism with 2 linear axes. Usually allows you to move the robot along X-Y axes.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Portal (3 axes)</strong> -     <span style="color: #000000;">
portal,     </span>
<span style="color: #000000;">
mechanism with     </span>
<span style="color: #000000;">
3 linear axes. Usually allows you to move the robot along X-Y-Z axes.    </span>
</span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">Machine equipment - </strong>Universal equipment with the possibility of configuration.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p><strong class=""> <span style="color: #000000;">
Parametric box -     </span>
</strong> <span style="color: #000000;">
Cube, with the possibility of changing its length, width, height.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p><strong class=""> <span style="color: #000000;">
Parametric table -     </span>
</strong> <span style="color: #000000;">
Table, with the possibility of changing its length, width, height, thickness and height of legs.    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p><strong class=""> <span style="color: #000000;">
Parametric turn-mill Machining centre -     </span>
</strong> <span style="color: #000000;">
Parametric machine (Turn).    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p><strong class=""> <span style="color: #000000;">
Parametric vertical Machining centre -     </span>
</strong> <span style="color: #000000;">
Parametric machine (Mill).    </span>
</p>
</li><li class=""><p> <span style="color: #000000;">
<strong class="">From file </strong>- allows you to add a mechanism exported to    <span style="color: #003366;">
 .mmm file. Check "Mechanism library" section for more information about .mmm files    </span>
</span>
</p>
</li></ul>
