# Assembly Simulation and Validation

We recommend validating the complete assembly by simulating mechanisms movements. Click ![Assembly Simulation and Validation](../assets/9ac21e84583e27626a9ee5fa3ab21f459ca8fde608bda3f5ac448a6727fefc39.png) button to open Assembly simulation panel.

![Assembly Simulation and Validation](../assets/be48a4c4382d09aac874c03c88b43695a3ffa768e9d01524e07ebe3aa63d3127.png)

In this tab there is "Enable collisions detection".

![Assembly Simulation and Validation](../assets/e85fad033ab3899a7c28a449295ced41e930c84b493e538fad79c0c1519268c6.png)

![Assembly Simulation and Validation](../assets/fbdb0d596161701fb5f17d030f770a9b494fa95ae536914bba8837a4eb0dca63.webp)

This panel is similar to the the panel described here: [Mechanism movements in simulation](131903767.md), but allows you to simulate all mechanisms in assembly.
