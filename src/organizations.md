# Organizations and collaborators

Two ways to publish together:

- **An organization** publishes for a company. The company accepts the terms once, and its
  members publish on its behalf.
- **Collaborators** help with one of your own extensions. Each of them is a developer in their own
  right.

## Create an organization

**Your company.** If your account of the CAM system belongs to a company and at least one
colleague from it also uses the store, **Account settings** → **Organizations** offers it under
**Your company**. Click **Bring it in**: you become the owner, and everybody whose account is in
this company is a member without an invitation.

**Another company.** Click **New organization**, enter the company name as the store should show
it, and click **Create**.

Then, in both cases:

1. Click **Fill in the company details**. The wizard is the one of
   [Becoming a developer](getting-started.md#become-a-developer), without the **Type** step: you
   fill in your details as the company representative and the company details.
2. Accept the terms on behalf of the company with **I Agree**. You confirm that you may bind it.
3. Invite the people who publish with you and are not members by company: enter the email of each
   in the people table and click **Invite**. A person can be invited once they have signed in to
   the store at least once.

On the card the organization is named by its public developer name. An organization that has not
accepted the terms and has no extensions can be deleted with **Delete**.

## Join an organization

If your account belongs to the company, you are a member as soon as the organization is in the
store, without an invitation, and you stop being one when your account leaves the company.

Anybody else is invited. An invitation appears in **Account settings** → **Organizations** →
**Invitations**; the store sends no email. **Accept** makes you a member, **Decline** refuses.
**Leave** takes you out.

Members do not sign the organization's agreement and need no developer registration of their
own: the owner's acceptance covers what they publish for it.

## Publish for an organization

Upload the package and choose the organization under **Publish as** on the form (see
[Publishing](publishing.md#the-publication-form)). A new extension then belongs to the
organization; a new version of an existing extension always goes to its owner. The GitHub route
publishes in your own name.

## Move your extension into an organization

In **My published**, click **Move into an organization** in the row of your own extension, choose
the organization and click **Move**. Its members then publish its versions, and the owner of the
organization manages it. Your collaborators leave the extension.

- An extension with prices or sales cannot be moved.
- A version on its way to the store must be published or withdrawn first.
- The organization must have accepted the terms.

You cannot take the extension back yourself. Only the owner of the organization can take it out
and hand it to a member, who must have accepted the developer terms as an individual.

The organization's extensions appear in **My published** of every member under the heading of the
organization.

| | Owner | Member |
|---|---|---|
| Publish new versions, hide and show, change the category | Yes | Yes |
| Company details, terms, invitations, removing people | Yes | No |
| Prices | Yes | No |

Organizations cannot sell yet: payouts are made to people (see
[Selling and payouts](paid-extensions.md#get-ready-to-sell)).

## Hand over the organization

The owner clicks **Make owner** in the row of a member. The member finds the offer under
**Invitations** and clicks **Accept**; until then the organization stays with the owner, who can
**Withdraw** the offer. The previous owner becomes a member.

## Collaborators

Invite a co-author to one of your own extensions: **My published** → **Collaborators** in its
row → enter their email → **Invite**.

A collaborator must be a developer: they accept the terms as individuals first, in **Account
settings** → **Developer**. They find the invitation in **Account settings** → **Organizations**
and click **Accept**. Then they publish new versions of the extension and hide or show it; prices,
money and the list of collaborators stay yours. **Remove** in the same window, or **Leave** on
their side, ends the collaboration.

Collaborators publish by uploading the package: the GitHub route publishes only extensions of
your own.
