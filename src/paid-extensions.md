# Paid extensions

## The paid profile

Every developer starts with the free profile. To publish paid extensions, choose **Free and
paid extensions** at registration and provide tax and payout details. The paid profile is open to
companies and to individuals acting for their trade, business or profession. Paid publishing
opens once the store team verifies the details; the review takes up to five business days. Until
then you publish free extensions.

A free extension must stay free in substance: no keys, subscriptions or in-extension payments
sold around the store (see [Store rules and your obligations](store-rules.md#free-means-free)).

## Marking an extension as paid

- On the upload form, select **Paid extension (requires a license)**.
- In the package, set `"licensed": true` in `package.info.json`.

An extension marked as paid stays paid in all its later versions: the mark cannot be removed.
Users who got a version while it was free keep using it, with its defect and security fixes, free
of charge; you may charge for new versions with new functions.

## What users see

- The card shows the **Paid** mark.
- Downloading and installing the extension requires a licence for it.
- A signed-in user can get a 14-day trial licence from the card (**Get 14-day trial**).

## Money

The Commercial Terms of the store govern prices, commission, payouts, refunds and taxes (see
[External references](references.md#store-terms)). In brief:

- Free extensions are published and distributed without any fee.
- For paid extensions the store retains a 30% commission on the transaction value, excluding
  taxes; the rest accrues to you.
- The store acts as the seller to users: it invoices them and accounts for VAT and sales tax. Your
  own taxes on what you receive are yours.
- Refunds are administered by the store and deducted from your balance.
