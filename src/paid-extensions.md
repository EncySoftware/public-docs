# Selling and payouts

Selling is optional: most extensions are free. If you want to charge, this page takes you from
the first switch to money in your bank account.

## Licence check and price

Two separate settings decide what users pay:

- **Licence check** — the extension runs only with a licence from the store: bought, a trial or
  given free. It is set by `"licensed": true` in the package or by **Paid** on the publication
  form, and it stays on every later version.
- **Price** — the plans users can buy and the length of the trial. You set them on the publication
  form of a paid version or later in **My published**.

| Licence check | Price | What users get |
|---|---|---|
| Off | — | A free extension: anyone installs it |
| On | Not set | Nothing yet: no trial and no purchase; the card says **Price not set** |
| On | **Free** | Anyone takes a free perpetual licence with one click |
| On | Plans | A trial, then a purchase or a subscription |

Setting a price turns the licence check on for every later version. Users who installed a version
while it was free keep using it.

A free extension must be free in substance (see
[Store rules and your obligations](store-rules.md#free-means-free)).

## Get ready to sell

1. **Paid profile.** Choose **Free and paid extensions** when you become a developer, or click
   **Enable paid extensions** later: on the publication form or in **Account settings** →
   **Developer**. The paid profile is open to companies and to individuals acting for their
   trade, business or profession.
2. **Payouts.** Click **Set up payouts** on the publication form or in **Account settings** →
   **Earnings**, and enter your bank and identity details on the Stripe form; the store never sees
   them. If Stripe needs more, **Continue setup at Stripe** takes you back. Payouts are open to
   companies incorporated, and individuals living, in the United States, the United Kingdom, the
   EEA, Canada or Switzerland.
3. **Approval.** Submit a paid version with your payouts ready: the moderator's approval enables
   paid extensions for you, and the extension comes out with its price. If you price an extension
   that is already published, the store team enables paid extensions once your payouts are ready.

The publication form and the price window walk you through the same steps: if something is
missing, they say what and offer the button for the next step.

Extensions of an organization cannot be sold yet: payouts are made to people, not to
organizations.

## Set a price

**With a paid version.** On the publication form choose **Paid** under **Pricing**, tick the
plans and enter the trial (see below). The price goes on the card the moment the version is
approved and reaches the feed. The form asks for plans only while the extension has no price;
after that the price stays as it is and changes only in **My published**.

**For a published extension.**

1. In **My published**, click the **Price** cell of the extension.
2. Choose **Paid**, tick the plans and enter the trial.
3. Click **Save price**.

The plans:

- **One-time** — a perpetual licence, from $19;
- **Monthly** — a subscription, from $10 a month;
- **Yearly** follows from the monthly price: ten monthly payments, two months free.

**Trial** — how many days users try the extension before they buy: from 14 to 30. Leave it empty
to use the store's default, which the field shows. A user gets the trial once.

Prices are in US dollars, up to $10,000. Users see every plan you tick and choose one. Only the
owner sets prices; members of an organization and collaborators see them but cannot change them.

**Changing a price.** New buyers pay the new price. Running subscriptions keep the price they
were bought at, and perpetual licences stay as they are. A plan you untick disappears for new
buyers; subscriptions bought on it keep renewing.

**Free.** Choose **Free** to give the extension away. With the licence check on, anyone gets a
free perpetual licence on **Get it free**, and keeps it if you set a price later.

**Free for ENCY resellers** and **Free for the ENCY team.** With the licence check on, these ticks
in the price window give the chosen group a licence for a year, renewed while they stay in the
group. It is not a sale: nothing accrues to you. Untick to stop new ones; licences already given
run out their year.

**Your own licence.** When a paid version of your extension reaches the feed, you get a perpetual
licence for it. Members of the organization and collaborators take theirs with **Get my license**
on the card.

## Your share

- The store keeps a 30% commission on each sale and renewal; 70% of the price accrues to you.
- The store sells to users and charges them; taxes on what you receive are yours.
- Refunds are made by the store team (see [What your users see](users.md#refunds)). A refund or a
  lost card dispute takes your share of that sale back.

The Commercial Terms govern prices, commission, payouts, refunds and taxes (see
[External references](references.md#store-terms)).

## Payouts

**Account settings** → **Earnings** shows your balance, your payouts and the history of your
sales and renewals.

- **Monthly payout.** When the balance reaches $100, it goes out in the monthly payout at the
  start of the next month, after the store team confirms the batch. A balance under $100 is paid
  out at the turn of the year.
- **First payout.** No earlier than 60 days after your first sale.
- **Early payout.** **Pay me now** sends the balance before the monthly payout, for a fee of $5
  plus 1% of the amount. It needs at least $50 on the balance and waits for the store team.
- **Checks.** Every payout passes a sanctions check before it leaves.
- **To your bank.** The store transfers the money to your Stripe account; Stripe pays it to your
  bank on its own schedule. **Open your Stripe dashboard** shows the transfers.

A payout that fails or is held back stays on your balance, and **Earnings** says why.
