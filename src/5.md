# 3D navigation

It is recommended to use a 3-buttons mouse with a scroll wheel for a full experience in MachineMaker and 3D navigation.

## Interactive rotate

Press and hold the right mouse button and drag the cursor around for the view rotation in the main visualization screen. Drag the cursor up and down to rotate around the horizontal screen axis. Drag it left and right to rotate around the vertical screen axis.

Use ![image2021-5-7_14-29-59](assets/6d3ced18200b08782d732d05ada41d510d8e08a8273bd7eb1d3f418db748a4c5.png) button to switch between free and Z-Fixed rotation modes.

Use ![image2023-10-27_15-55-42](assets/271b6c6f7b4b5c47016f9f9c812812697835da60260de226ee340696ea8202b2.png) the measure tool to measure the distance.

[![Lathemillingmachine](assets/6021a464e706c7dba38fb1e3942245d3dca87adef599b2b760d864e5b96ebbaf.gif)](assets/6021a464e706c7dba38fb1e3942245d3dca87adef599b2b760d864e5b96ebbaf.gif "Open full-size image")

Changing the viewpoint can also be done by the ***Standard views*** buttons.

[![image2021-5-7_14-20-38](assets/bc79c6bc758ba4400b233576020ccadb7a4950fe26d550c6f447cddf9a00c377.png)](assets/bc79c6bc758ba4400b233576020ccadb7a4950fe26d550c6f447cddf9a00c377.png "Open full-size image")

You can also use interactive ***Global CS*** located in the lower left corner of the main window.[![LatheMillingMachineGlobalCS](assets/e9e4fb475a0ab8ca2816c71186503b50c1ee17a01b8ba0f7bef74e0dac1bb8e0.gif)](assets/e9e4fb475a0ab8ca2816c71186503b50c1ee17a01b8ba0f7bef74e0dac1bb8e0.gif "Open full-size image")

Clicking on the ***Middle Mouse Button (wheel)*** in the graphics window rotates the current view to the nearest standard plane.

## Interactive pan

Press and hold the ***Middle Mouse Button (wheel)*** and drag the cursor in the main visualization screen to move the view vector.

## Interactive zoom in and out

Use ***Mouse Wheel Scroll*** to zoom. The zoom area is where your mouse cursor is located.

## Visualization mode

It is possible to change visualization mode in the Mechanism edit mode.[![визуализациятокфрез](assets/39a3553b8792cd888ac7c53ba18b5f9e901934b075a54f015cabaabf4a816132.gif)](assets/39a3553b8792cd888ac7c53ba18b5f9e901934b075a54f015cabaabf4a816132.gif "Open full-size image")
