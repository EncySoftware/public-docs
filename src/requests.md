# Requests from customers

Customers ask DMC for components that are not in the catalog, or for changes to ones that are — a
post processor for their machine, a fourth axis on a machine schema. The DMC team handles these
requests, and passes one on to you when it is about a component of yours.

## Requests for me

A request passed on to you appears in **Requests for me** in your account, and in the
notifications bell. It shows what the customer asked and what the DMC team wants from you; who asked
stays with DMC.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Waiting for you** | Passed on to you and not answered yet. |
| **Answered** | You answered it, and the customer was told where to find your component. |
| **Closed by DMC** | The DMC team closed it. |

## Answering a request

1. Click **Answer…** on the request.
2. Pick the component of yours that answers it — usually the one it was asked about, updated.
3. Click **Answer and notify**. The customer gets a link to it.

Only your published components can answer a request: publish the new or updated component first
(see [Review, updates and testing](review-and-updates.md)).
