# Curve on a surface

`CreateCurveOnSurface(ID: string; CurveID, SurfaceID: string)` — create a 3D curve lying on a surface. The source curve is defined in the parametric space of the surface and projected onto it.

- `ID` — identifier of the entity being created;
- `CurveID` — identifier of the source curve;
- `SurfaceID` — identifier of the carrier surface.
