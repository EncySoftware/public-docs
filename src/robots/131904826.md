# Examples: Robot cell

In this chapter, you will find instructional videos detailing the process of adding and configuring various components in the software. The covered topics include:

1. **Simple Robot**

   - Instruction on adding a simple robot.
2. **Heavy-Duty Robot**

   - Guidance on incorporating heavy-duty robot models.
3. **Cobot (Collaborative Robot)**

   - Instructions for adding and configuring collaborative robot models.
4. **End Effectors**

   - Tutorial on the addition and setup of end effectors.
5. **End Effector TCP (Tool Center Point)**

   - Demonstrations on adding and configuring end effector TCP.
6. **External Tools**

   - Guidance on adding and configuring external tools.
7. **Composite End Effector**

   - Tutorial covering the addition and setup of composite end effectors.
8. **Composite End Effector: Spot Welding**

   - Specific tutorial on adding and configuring spot welding composite end effectors.
9. **Tables and User Coordinate Systems (CS)**

   - Instructional content on adding and configuring tables and user coordinate systems.
10. **Rail**

    - Tutorial on adding rail components and configuring their settings.
11. **H-Bot (Portal)**

    - Instructional video covering the addition and configuration of H-Bot (Portal) systems.
12. **SCARA Robot**

    - Instructions for adding and configuring SCARA robot models.
