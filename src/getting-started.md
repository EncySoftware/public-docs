# Getting started

## What you need

- An ENCY account. DMC has no separate sign-up: **Sign in** on the DMC website uses the same
  account as ENCY.
- The Publisher role in DMC (see [The Publisher role](#the-publisher-role)).
- ENCY, to try every component before you publish it: customers get the component exactly as you
  upload it.
- For AI publishing only: the .NET 8 SDK or newer, and Cursor, Claude Code, Codex or another AI
  assistant that runs MCP servers (see [Publishing](publishing.md#from-an-ai-assistant)).

## The Publisher role

DMC has three roles — Customer, Publisher and Admin — and none includes another. Every new account
starts as Customer: it can browse the catalog, request components and get licences. Publishing
needs the Publisher role.

- **Staff of an ENCY dealer** ask for it in DMC. In your account, open **Profile**, find
  **Publisher access** and click **Request access**. Add a note on what you would like to publish if
  you wish, and click **Send request**. The card reads **Pending** until the DMC team answers.
- **Everyone else** writes to the DMC team (see [Contacts](references.md#contacts)).

With the role, the DMC header shows a **Publish** button, and your account gets three more
sections:

- **My published** — every component you own, whatever its status. You upload, edit and submit
  here; **Publish** opens it.
- **Bulk import** — ZIP archives in, many drafts out.
- **Requests for me** — customers' requests about your components that the DMC team passes on to
  you (see [Requests from customers](requests.md)).

## Choose a route

Nothing goes to review until you submit it: **Submit** in the form sends a new component straight
there, and every other route creates drafts.

| Route | Best for | Where it is described |
|---|---|---|
| **The form** — **Upload asset** in **My published** | One component at a time | [Publishing](publishing.md#from-the-form) |
| **ZIP import** — **Bulk import** | Many components already in folders or archives | [Publishing](publishing.md#from-zip-archives) |
| **CSV import** — **Upload CSV** in **My published** | Many components listed in a spreadsheet | [Publishing](publishing.md#from-a-spreadsheet) |
| **AI assistant** — dmc-mcp | Publishing, correcting and linking from Cursor, Claude Code or Codex | [Publishing](publishing.md#from-an-ai-assistant) |

## First publication checklist

- You have the Publisher role.
- The component works in your ENCY, in the versions you are going to tick in **ENCY ver**.
- Its file is packed the way DMC reads it (see [Components and archives](components-and-archives.md));
  a machine schema's archive holds ENCY's preview picture (see
  [Covers](the-card.md#covers)).
- The card has the five fields a submission needs: **Product Name**, the machine's
  **Manufacturer**, **Machine Type**, **Product File** and **Price** (see
  [The card and links](the-card.md#fields-needed-to-submit)).
- **Price** is the one you mean: **Generic**, **With Maintenance** or **Paid**. A component from the
  form, a ZIP import or an AI assistant starts as **Generic** — free and permanent — until you
  change it (see [Access, prices and licences](licences-and-prices.md)).
- The component is linked to the ones it goes with — a post processor to the machine schemas it was
  made for (see [The card and links](the-card.md#links-to-other-components)).
