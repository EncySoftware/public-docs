# Digital Machine Center for publishers

Digital Machine Center (DMC) is the catalog of CAM components for ENCY: post processors, machine
schemas, interpreters and Digital Machine Kits. Customers find them on the DMC website or on the
**Digital Machine Center** tab in ENCY, get a licence on the component's card, and ENCY downloads
the component for them. This guide is for publishers: it takes you from a component that works in
your ENCY to one published in the catalog, and through its later updates.

Writing the components themselves — post processors, machine schemas, interpreters — is covered by
their own documentation; this guide links to it instead of repeating it (see
[External references](references.md)).

## The path in brief

1. Get the Publisher role for your ENCY account.
2. Pack each component the way DMC reads it: a post processor's file, a machine schema's folder, an
   interpreter's files, or a kit of them.
3. Upload it as a draft: in the form, as ZIP archives, from a spreadsheet, or from an AI assistant.
4. Complete the card — machine, controller, price, links — and submit it for review.
5. Wait for the DMC team to publish it. DMC tries every machine schema and kit in ENCY by itself;
   later changes to a published component go live without a second review.
