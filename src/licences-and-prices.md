# Access, prices and licences

## Access

**Price** on the card decides how customers get the component:

| In the form | In the catalog | What a customer gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Generic** | **Generic** | A free, permanent licence. |
| **With Maintenance** | **Available with active Maintenance** | A licence for as long as their ENCY maintenance runs. A customer without active maintenance is refused. |
| **Paid**, with an amount in € | **Paid · € 490** | A 14-day trial, for a customer with active ENCY maintenance. To buy, the customer asks for a commercial quote. |

- A customer gets one trial per component.
- The quote is asked for from the licence in **My licenses**, and the dealer team comes back to the
  customer with the price.
- A customer may take up to 10 components a month that are not Generic, and an ENCY dealer's
  company up to 30. Generic components are not counted, and your own components never count against
  you.

## How customers get a component

Customers press **Get License** on the component's card — on the DMC website or on the
**Digital Machine Center** tab in ENCY (**Clouds** on the ENCY start page). On a paid component the
button reads **Try for 14 days** on the website and **Get Trial** in ENCY. ENCY then saves the
licence, activates it and downloads the component.

A licence holder keeps the component when you later disable or archive it, and gets every new
archive you upload (see
[Review, updates and testing](review-and-updates.md#updating-a-published-component)).

## Your statistics

In **My published**:

- **Licenses** — how many licences were issued. Click the number for **License details**: the
  totals (**Total**, **Active**, **Expired**, **Trial**) and the 50 latest licences with their type,
  status and dates.
- **Downloads** — how many people downloaded the component.
