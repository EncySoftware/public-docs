# Seleção inteligente de objetos

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-13-9.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-13-9.png)

## Área de aplicação:

A seleção inteligente economiza tempo ao selecionar elementos de uma peça segundo regras específicas.

Use os seguintes atalhos para ativar cada algoritmo:

**Clique duplo** em um elemento do modelo 3D.

<details>
<summary>Clique aqui para expandir.</summary>

- Um clique duplo em uma aresta 3D seleciona todas as arestas tangentes.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-13-9.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-13-9.png)

- Um clique duplo em um círculo seleciona todos os círculos com o mesmo diâmetro.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-24-29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-24-29.png)

- Um clique duplo em um segmento de contorno seleciona todos os segmentos desse contorno.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/GeomModelIntellSelect2.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/GeomModelIntellSelect2.png)

- Um clique duplo em uma face cilíndrica interna ou em um furo seleciona todos os cilindros internos com o mesmo diâmetro.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-20-57.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-20-57.png)

- Um clique duplo em uma face cilíndrica externa seleciona todas as faces cilíndricas com o mesmo diâmetro e eixo.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-26-17.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-26-17.png)

- Um clique duplo em uma face plana seleciona todas as faces coplanares.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-28-20.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-28-20.png)

</details>

**Shift + clique duplo** em um objeto do modelo 3D.

<details>
<summary>Clique aqui para expandir.</summary>

- Seleciona todos os cilindros internos com o mesmo diâmetro em um determinado plano.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-32-8.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-32-8.png)

- Na definição da tarefa de contorno 6D, permite selecionar arestas de tamanho semelhante.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-52-44.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-52-44.png)

- Permite selecionar arestas não tangentes. A seleção depende da posição da peça na janela gráfica.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-54-58.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-54-58.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-56-47.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_15-56-47.png)

</details>

**Ctrl + Shift + clique duplo** em um objeto do modelo 3D.

<details>
<summary>Clique aqui para expandir.</summary>

Permite selecionar superfícies com continuidade suave.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_17-21-15.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_17-21-15.png)

</details>

Clique com o **botão direito** em uma superfície para ativar **Propagation**.

<details>
<summary>Clique aqui para expandir.</summary>

Além dos elementos selecionados, os filtros de contexto permitem ajustar a seleção.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-45-43.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-45-43.png)

Elementos geométricos básicos disponíveis:

- **Fillet** — concordância arredondada.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-52-22.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-52-22.png)

- **Chamfer** — chanfro.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-56-49.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-56-49.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-57-11.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-57-11.png)

- **Pocket** — cavidade; selecione seu fundo.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-6-2_14-50-54.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-6-2_14-50-54.png)

- **Side** — lateral.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-6-2_14-51-46.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-6-2_14-51-46.png)

- **Boss** — saliência; selecione seu plano superior.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-6-2_14-52-1.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-6-2_14-52-1.png)

- **Smooth** — superfícies com união suave.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_17-12-48.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_17-12-48.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_17-21-152.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_17-21-152.png)

- **Hole** — furo.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-57-48.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-57-48.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-58-0.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-5-29_16-58-0.png)

Na seleção de furos, estão disponíveis os filtros:

- **By shape** — por forma.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-7-14_9-13-33.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-7-14_9-13-33.png)

- **Plane and shape** — por plano e forma.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-7-14_9-13-22.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-7-14_9-13-22.png)

Na seleção de superfícies, está disponível o filtro:

- **By Color.** Selecione uma superfície, clique em **Propagate** e ative **By Color**. O sistema seleciona todas as superfícies do modelo 3D com a mesma cor.

![Selecting a surface and enabling By Color](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/intellectual-selection-by-color.png)

![Selecting all surfaces with the same color](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/intellectual-selection-by-color-result.png)

- **Outer loop.** Seleciona automaticamente as arestas externas da superfície escolhida.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-7-14_9-15-59.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-7-14_9-15-59.png)

- **Custom Loop.** Permite controlar o sentido do contorno, aresta por aresta, em cada bifurcação encontrada ao percorrer o modelo. Use as teclas ◀ e ▶.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2026-8-11_11-25-26.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2026-8-11_11-25-26.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2026-8-11_11-29-12.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2026-8-11_11-29-12.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2026-8-11_11-31-3.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2026-8-11_11-31-3.png)

</details>

Elementos geométricos básicos disponíveis:

**Tangent Edges.** Identifica e seleciona automaticamente arestas em contato tangencial no modelo 3D.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-34-29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-34-29.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-34-46.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-34-46.png)

**Loop1.** Seleciona cadeias consecutivas e fechadas de arestas segundo a primeira variante de percurso.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-34-29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-34-29.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-35-10.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-35-10.png)

**Loop2.** Seleciona cadeias consecutivas e fechadas de arestas segundo a segunda variante de percurso.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-34-29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-34-29.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-35-29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-35-29.png)

**Enclosed Faces.** Seleciona as superfícies dentro de um contorno fechado.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-35-54.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-35-54.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-36-11.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-17_12-36-11.png)

**Outer Adjacent Faces.** Seleciona as superfícies adjacentes pelo lado externo de um contorno fechado.

![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-22_12-24-21.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-22_12-24-21.png) ![images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-22_12-24-35.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157859368/image2025-10-22_12-24-35.png)

</details>

Use **Ctrl + Alt + botão esquerdo do mouse** sobre uma superfície para ativar a seleção por pintura (**Surface selection via painting**).
