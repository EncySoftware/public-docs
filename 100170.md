# Basic

## Application area:

The **Basic** group contains face milling, waterline and plane machining, contouring, and flat-area finishing operations. These topics describe the common milling strategies and their operation settings.

### Operations:

- **Face milling.** Machines planar faces. [Details](10117.md)

- **Roughing waterline.** Removes stock in horizontal layers. [Details](736.md)

- **Finishing waterline.** Finishes surfaces using waterline passes. [Details](841.md)

- **Roughing plane.** Removes stock using plane passes. [Details](735.md)

- **2.5D contouring.** Machines contours at different levels. [Details](10115.md)

- **Flat land.** Finishes flat areas of the model. [Details](759.md)

- **3D contouring.** Machines spatial curves. [Details](700.md)
