# Space curves

The **Space curve** button creates a curve using the method selected from its menu. The curve is linked to its source surface or profile and follows changes to it.

**Break links.** Makes the curve permanently independent. The source can then be deleted, but the curve can no longer trim the surface.

## Creation methods

**Surface edge.** Hover over a surface edge to highlight it, then click to create the curve. **Edge** selects one of the four edges.

**Composite curve.** Click curves to add links, including curves from different planes and space curves. Click a link again to remove it. Finish by double-clicking the last link, closing the chain or pressing **[Enter]**. **[Esc]** discards the selection. **Tolerance** sets the construction tolerance.

**Isoparametric curve.** Move the pointer over a surface to highlight the nearest isocurve, then click. **Isocurve** selects **Along** or **Across**; **Parameter** ranges from 0 to 1.

**Projected curve.** Click a planar profile, then a sketch or part surface. Projection follows the normal to the profile plane. **Tolerance** sets the projection tolerance.

**Segment by two points.** Creates a line segment between node points.

**Cut surfaces by contour.** Select a profile: the preview shows its projection onto visible surfaces. Click a surface piece to choose the side to remove. Projections and trimming of all affected surfaces are created as one undo step.

## Common properties

**Definition**, **Method**. Identify how the curve is defined and constructed.

**Face parameters.** Read-only information indicating whether a boundary taken from a model face retains that face's parameters.

**Independent (no links).** The state after breaking links.

## Span of curve

**Split object at point** creates two linked spans of a space curve and hides the original curve. **Range** shows the span as a percentage of the original curve length. In contrast, splitting a planar profile creates two independent objects. [Details](20023.md)
