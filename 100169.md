# 2D

## Application area:

The **2D** group contains operations for machining pockets, engraving, and following planar contours. The operation topics describe the geometry and parameters required to generate each toolpath.

### Operations:

- **Pocketing.** Removes material from pockets defined by the job assignment. [Details](256.md)

- **Engraving.** Machines engraved geometry and inscriptions. [Details](810.md)

- **2D contouring.** Machines planar contours. [Details](404.md)
