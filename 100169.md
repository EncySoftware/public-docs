# 2D

## Application area:

The <strong>2D</strong> group contains operations for machining pockets, engraving, and following planar contours. The operation topics describe the geometry and parameters required to generate each toolpath.

### Operations:

- <strong>Pocketing.</strong> Removes material from pockets defined by the job assignment. [Details](256.md)

- <strong>Engraving.</strong> Machines engraved geometry and inscriptions. [Details](810.md)

- <strong>2D contouring.</strong> Machines planar contours. [Details](404.md)
