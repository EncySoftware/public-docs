# Bar feeding

![images/download/attachments/157866480/Bar.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866480/Bar.png)

## Application area:

The *bar feeding* operation is essential in modern machining for automating the production process. Its main purpose is to continuously and automatically feed long metal bars into a machine tool — most commonly a CNC turning centre or lathe.

## Setup:

The **Setup** tab is used to configure the primary parameters of the project. This can involve the positioning of the part on the equipment, the coordinate system of the part, and more. [See mor](1022.md)

### Start with target.

<details>

### WCS Use canned cycle.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**Enable.** The bar transferring process will be formalized as a cycle for the further analysis in the postprocessor

**Disable.** The bar transferring process will be output as a sequence of elementary commands

</details>

### Physic output.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**Enable.** **Physicgoto** command and physic coordinates are used to move the machine axes

**Disable. Multigoto** or **Goto** commands and coordinates are generated to move the machine axes

</details>

### Initial clamp

The clamping device that initially holds the part. Special CLData commands for clamping/unclamping the device are output automatically. **Parameter group works similarly to the Turn take over operations**. [Details](10406.md)
