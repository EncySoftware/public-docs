# Mill machining

## Application area:

<strong>Mill machining</strong> provides operations for removing material and finishing part surfaces with a rotating cutting tool. Operations are organized into groups matching the operation selection window.

### Operation groups:

- <strong>Holes.</strong> Provides operations for machining holes and generating machining processes from recognized manufacturing features. [Details](100168.md)

- <strong>2D.</strong> Contains operations for machining pockets, engraving, and following planar contours. The operation topics describe the geometry and parameters required to generate each toolpath. [Details](100169.md)

- <strong>Basic.</strong> Contains face milling, waterline and plane machining, contouring, and flat-area finishing operations. These topics describe the common milling strategies and their operation settings. [Details](100170.md)

- <strong>Advanced.</strong> Contains operations for surface finishing, undercut machining, multi-axis roughing, and machining spatial contours. The individual topics describe the available toolpath strategies and tool orientation settings. [Details](100171.md)

- <strong>Rotary.</strong> Contains operations that use a rotary axis for machining surfaces and contours. The topics cover rotary roughing and finishing, waterline machining, morphing, surfacing, and contouring. [Details](100172.md)

- <strong>Turbomachinery Milling.</strong> Contains specialized operations for machining impellers and individual blades. The operation topics describe the required blade and hub geometry, machining strategies, and toolpath settings. [Details](100173.md)

- <strong>Rest machining.</strong> Contains corners cleanup, pencil, and chamfering operations. [Details](727.md)

### Operation setup:

Common operation settings and topics covering machining with different numbers of axes are described in <strong>Operations setup</strong>. [Details](10149.md)
