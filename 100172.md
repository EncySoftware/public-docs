# Rotary

## Application area:

The <strong>Rotary</strong> group contains operations that use a rotary axis for machining surfaces and contours. The topics cover rotary roughing and finishing, waterline machining, morphing, surfacing, and contouring.

### Operations:

- <strong>Rotary waterline.</strong> Generates waterline passes around a rotary axis. [Details](10143.md)

- <strong>Rotary roughing.</strong> Removes stock in layers around a rotary axis. [Details](10137.md)

- <strong>Rotary finishing.</strong> Finishes surfaces around a rotary axis. [Details](10136.md)

- <strong>Morph 4D.</strong> Generates four-axis morphing toolpaths. [Details](10146.md)

- <strong>4D Surfacing.</strong> Generates four-axis surface machining toolpaths. [Details](10145.md)

- <strong>4D Contouring.</strong> Generates four-axis contouring toolpaths. [Details](10144.md)
