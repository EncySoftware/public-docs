# Rotary

## Application area:

The **Rotary** group contains operations that use a rotary axis for machining surfaces and contours. The topics cover rotary roughing and finishing, waterline machining, morphing, surfacing, and contouring.

### Operations:

- **Rotary waterline.** Generates waterline passes around a rotary axis. [Details](10143.md)

- **Rotary roughing.** Removes stock in layers around a rotary axis. [Details](10137.md)

- **Rotary finishing.** Finishes surfaces around a rotary axis. [Details](10136.md)

- **Morph 4D.** Generates four-axis morphing toolpaths. [Details](10146.md)

- **4D Surfacing.** Generates four-axis surface machining toolpaths. [Details](10145.md)

- **4D Contouring.** Generates four-axis contouring toolpaths. [Details](10144.md)
