# Operations setup

This section describes how to configure jet cutting operations, from assigning the geometry to selecting machining conditions and strategy settings. It covers contour selection, machining direction, cutting side, and the properties of job assignment items.

The topics also explain settings shared by wire EDM and 4D jet cutting, including synchronization of upper and lower contours, approaches and returns, bridges, and compensation. Use this section when preparing the job assignment and adjusting the cutting sequence and toolpath behavior.
