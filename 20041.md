# Soldagem por pontos

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-24-47.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-24-47.png)

A operação pode ser usada para ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-7-45.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-7-45.png) <strong>Tack weld</strong> (solda de fixação) e ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-8-5.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-8-5.png) <strong>Spot weld</strong> (solda a ponto).

#### Definição da tarefa

Na guia de estratégia, selecione o tipo de soldagem: ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-7-45.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-7-45.png) Tack weld

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2024-7-17_17-40-29.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2024-7-17_17-40-29.png)

![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-8-5.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-8-5.png) Spot weld

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2024-7-17_17-41-20.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2024-7-17_17-41-20.png)

Para ambos os tipos, a definição da tarefa é a mesma.

A <strong>definição da tarefa ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-9-9.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-9-9.png)</strong> consiste em dois tipos de pontos:

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2024-7-17_17-42-33.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2024-7-17_17-42-33.png)

![images/download/attachments/157866499/weld.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/weld.png) Weld Point (ponto de solda) ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/Move.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/Move.png) Move Point (ponto de movimentação)

Esses pontos podem ser usados para construir uma cadeia de trajetórias:

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_16-56-19.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_16-56-19.png)

<strong>Arrastar pontos</strong>

Esses pontos podem ser movidos arrastando-os.

Eles se fixam a faces ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-10-58.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-10-58.png), curvas ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-11-38.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-11-38.png), splines ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-11-38.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-11-38.png), arestas ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-12-21.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-12-21.png) e vértices ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-12-55.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-12-55.png). Ao se fixar, o eixo da ferramenta gira automaticamente.

Os <strong>Move points</strong> podem ser movidos facilmente com o <strong>Smart snap ![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-29-40.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-29-40.png)</strong>:

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-28-54.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-28-54.png)

A posição do ponto também pode ser especificada como um deslocamento em relação ao ponto auxiliar amarelo.

Para fazer isso, primeiro selecione o ponto amarelo:

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-41-28.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-41-28.png)

Você também pode girar o vetor do eixo arrastando o vetor visual:

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_17-7-31.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_17-7-31.png)
