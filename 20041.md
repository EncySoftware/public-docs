<strong>Drag points</strong>

These points can be moved by dragging.

They attach to faces ![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-10-58.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-10-58.png), curves![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-11-38.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-11-38.png), splines![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-11-38.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-11-38.png), edges![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-12-21.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-12-21.png), vertices![images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-12-55.png](assets/images/download/thumbnails/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-12-55.png). Tool axis rotates automatically when attached.

<strong>Move points</strong> can be easily moved using <strong>Smart snap ![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-29-40.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-29-40.png)</strong>:

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-28-54.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-16_18-28-54.png)

Position of the point can also be specified as an offset from the auxiliary yellow point.

To do this, first select the yellow point:

![images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-41-28.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157866499/image2022-5-20_10-41-28.png)

You can also rotate the axis vector by dragging the visual vector:
