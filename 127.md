# Selection filter

The object selection filter toolbar is located at the bottom of the right pane of the main window.

When choosing among the many geometric objects in the graphics window, it is useful to limit the selectability of objects depending on their type ([point, curve, mesh, surface, etc.](283.md)). Filter parameters can be set by pressing the corresponding buttons on the toolbar. By <strong>[Left Mouse Button]</strong> clicking the chosen button, the corresponding filter is toggled on and off.
