# Trim function

![images/download/attachments/157860407/image2024-8-9_17-19-48.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860407/image2024-8-9_17-19-48.png)

The function finds the intersection points of the specified element with others and highlights the area that will be deleted after confirmation by clicking.

![images/download/attachments/157860407/image2024-8-9_17-20-59.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860407/image2024-8-9_17-20-59.png)

After confirmation:

![images/download/attachments/157860407/image2024-8-9_17-21-48.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860407/image2024-8-9_17-21-48.png)

You can also cut fragments of several elements at once by pressing the mouse button and dragging.

![images/download/attachments/157860407/image2024-8-9_17-23-9.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157860407/image2024-8-9_17-23-9.png)

All intersections of objects with this imaginary line will be removed after releasing the mouse button:
