# Weaving

![images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Normal_to_surface.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Normal_to_surface.png)

## Application area:

Mechanical weaving is used, for example, to compensate for tolerances or to bridge gaps in a seam. The torch moves across the seam in this instance and the weave oscillation is thus superposed on the seam motion. It is also possible to rotate the torch in relation to the plane of the **weld** (direction of welding). Can also be used for **polishing.**

## Weaving.

**NC Code Format.**

Defines how the trajectory is output.

**Long Hang.** The trajectory is output with transformation according to the parameters.

**Canned Cycle.** The trajectory is output as lines from the work order. At the beginning of each section, the cycle start command is output, and at the end, the cycle end command. In order for this to work, it is necessary to register the corresponding commands in the postprocessor.

**Pattern.** Pattern defining the type of weaving.

<details>
<summary>Click here to expand..</summary>

**Triangle.** Deflection of the welding torch in 1 direction as Triangle.

![images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Triangle.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Triangle.png)

**Trapezoid.** Deflection of the welding torch in 1 direction with dual frequency.

![images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Trapecoid.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Trapecoid.png)

**Unsymetric Trapecoid.** Work with robot cycles.

**Spiral.** Deflection of the welding torch in 2 directions (amplitude = weave length/2).

![images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Spiral.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Spiral.png)

**Double 8.** Figure of eight weaving.

![images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Double_8.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Double_8.png)

**On Seam.** Work with robot cycles

**Edge Bottom.** Work with robot cycles

**Edge Top.** Work with robot cycles

**Curvy.** Сreates a curved zigzag toolpath (with arcs) on the piece.

![images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Curvy.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Curvy.png)

**Crescent.** Generates a template in the form сrescent.

![images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Crescent.png](assets/images/download/attachments/157862404/Weaving_Crescent.png)
